The five themes (content pillars)

Everything we publish maps to one of five themes. Each has an owner who guides the topics. Yours is the first one, and it runs through most of the plan.

  • AI-Native Delivery (yours). How we use AI to build software faster, and the AI we build into clients' products.
  • Product Thinking (Denisa). Building the right product: what to build, what to defer, what to kill.
  • Deep Domain Expertise (the delivery team, whoever knows the relevant industry best). Knowing finance, healthcare, cybersecurity, and education deeply enough to build for them.
  • Elite Engineering Talent (Kuba, pending your nod). How our senior engineers ramp fast and ship quality work.
  • Outsourcing (Jake). What working with a real engineering partner should look like, versus a cheap dev shop.
