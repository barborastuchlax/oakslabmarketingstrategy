OAK'S LAB Marketing Strategy

01 · The basics What we're building, who we serve, what we believe
Build sustainable inbound from CTOs and CPOs at high-growth finance, cybersecurity, healthcare, education, and B2B SaaS companies, by being findable when they search, recognizable when they research, and trusted when they shortlist.
Why now
OAK'S LAB grows through outbound and referrals. Both work, both have ceilings. Marketing's job is to build the second growth lever: inbound that compounds, doesn't leak when budget stops.
What we're aiming for
A measurable inbound engine producing 7+ qualified leads / month from ICP, sustained. Quality clears the bar BD can close. The engine gets stronger month over month.
How we get there
The engine below: Create / Capture / Convert. Each channel and asset serves one of three jobs: get attention, get found, persuade.

What we believe
01

Industry expertise beats general capability

Finance CTOs and CPOs hire finance-fluent partners. We win by being deep, not broad.

02

Long-term partnership beats project work

Our 4-year retention is the economic engine. We attract buyers who think in years.

03

Content compounds, paid leaks

Paid stops earning when it stops. Content built in 2026 still earns in 2028.

04

CTOs and CPOs are research-led, peer-validated

They respond to authority signals (case studies, named clients, technical depth) at the moments they're looking.

03 · How we'll know and what we won't do Measurement and constraints
How we'll know it's working
A sustainable inbound engine where pipeline quality clears the bar BD can actually close, and the engine compounds month over month, instead of leaking when paid budget stops.
Inbound becomes primary New conversations come increasingly from inbound. Less dependency on outbound bandwidth or referral velocity.
Pipeline quality clears the bar The leads that hit BD are real prospects: ICP-fit, intent-signaling, closeable. Not vanity volume.
Engine compounds Content built this quarter still earns next quarter. Authority signals stack. The system gets stronger without proportional new spend.
Operating target: 7 highly-qualified leads / month from ICP, sustained. Supporting metrics (volume, CPL, conversion rate, retention) serve this bar, not the other way around.
